```yaml

composition: one coherent physical system; use causal loops, process maps, tracks, or branches with an unmistakable reading path and generous outer margins
text-rules: exact high-contrast labels only on flat front-facing cards or tabs; exact visible text is exhaustive; keep every other surface blank
```

Treat relationships as physical construction: cards connect through tracks, tabs, bridges, or loops, and every material change must explain hierarchy or causality. This is not a generic editorial-material illustration with one decorative object; the complete system topology is the focal point.

Keep all labels horizontal and front-facing. Never place text on curved clay, angled paper edges, or deeply foreshortened surfaces.
